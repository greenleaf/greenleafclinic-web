Why I Do This Work

A different kind of listening,
carried from the classroom

Before acupuncture, Clare was a teacher. She spent years in Korean classrooms watching teenagers grow — not just taller, but into who they'd become. That's where she learned health is never just physical, a lesson she carried straight into her study of Traditional Chinese Medicine.

She believes pain deserves to be taken seriously at every stage of life — from growing pains in adolescence, to pain tied to women's health, to sports injuries, to pain following a car accident, to the aches that come with aging — because how pain is heard and addressed shapes how a person feels about their whole body for years to come. For her, every patient, at any age, deserves a diagnosis built around how they actually feel, not only what a chart says.

That's the practice she's built: questions are always welcome, treatment is only the beginning, and the real goal is a life patients don't need to keep coming back to fix.

Common Questions

New to acupuncture? Start here

Does acupuncture actually hurt?

Acupuncture needles are hair-thin — nothing like an injection needle. Most patients feel a light tap or a brief, dull sensation, if anything at all. Clare uses a gentle insertion technique and will always tell you what to expect before each point.

Are the needles safe and sterile?

Yes. Every needle is single-use, pre-sterilized, and disposed of immediately after treatment — standard practice required by CTCMPAO regulation, and non-negotiable at this clinic.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the condition and how long you've had it. Many acute issues respond within 3–6 sessions; longer-standing or chronic conditions usually need a longer plan. You'll get a realistic estimate after your first visit — not an open-ended commitment.

Is this covered by insurance or ICBC?

Most extended health plans in BC include acupuncture coverage — Clare will provide a receipt formatted for direct claims. If your visit relates to a motor vehicle accident, ask about ICBC-related coverage when booking.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first visit includes a full intake — history, current symptoms, tongue and pulse assessment — followed by your first treatment in the same session. Plan for about 45–60 minutes.

Is acupuncture safe for children or during pregnancy?

Yes, with adjusted technique. Pediatric treatment uses fewer, shallower needles (or non-insertive methods for younger children), and pregnancy-safe point selection avoids contraindicated points. Always mention pregnancy or your child's age when booking.
