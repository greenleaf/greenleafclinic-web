Four Patterns We Commonly See in Fertility Cases

TYPE 01 Poor Circulation Type (Cold Constitution)

Throughout the menstrual cycle, the body needs to send extra blood flow to the uterus. When peripheral circulation is chronically weak, the body spends its energy warming cold hands and feet first, leaving too little blood flow for the reproductive organs. Heat can also pool in the upper body and head, triggering headaches. Trying to fix circulation through exercise or saunas often backfires too, leaving you more depleted from fluid and electrolyte loss rather than actually warmer.

Signs your body may be showing

Cold lower abdomen and lower back
Craving warm food and drinks
Poor tolerance for cold environments
Frequent chills and body aches
Cold hands and feet
Headaches and dizziness
Pale complexion
Light, watery periods
Easy fatigue, low energy
Symptoms worsen around your period
TYPE 02 Hormonal Imbalance Type (PCOS)

PCOS is a complex hormonal imbalance where insulin resistance and elevated androgens are tangled together. It makes ovulation irregular and can lead to small cysts forming on the ovaries. Where conventional medicine tends to focus on managing symptoms, our approach works on regulating the body's own hormone-producing system, so ovulation and cycles can return to a regular rhythm.

Signs your body may be showing

Big mood swings
Frequent sugar cravings
Easily fatigued
Irregular or absent periods
Thick coating on the tongue
Difficulty managing weight
Oily skin and acne
Hair thinning or loss
Excess, coarse body hair
TYPE 03 Blood Stasis Type

Blood flow stagnates in the pelvis, so the reproductive organs aren't getting enough oxygen and nutrients. Clotted menstrual blood and stabbing pain are signs that circulation isn't flowing smoothly — and that can affect egg quality, implantation, and reproductive function overall.

Signs your body may be showing

Bruises easily
Sharp, stabbing pain in one fixed spot
Purplish lips or nail beds
Chronic constipation
Heavy, painful periods
Breast tenderness before your period
Dark, clotted menstrual blood
Severe cramping pain
Lower abdominal pain
History of endometriosis or uterine fibroids
TYPE 04 Stress Type

Chronic stress keeps the body in a constant state of tension and affects the hypothalamus, the part of the brain that directs hormone signaling. The result can be irregular ovulation or periods that stop altogether. Because digestion, sleep, and overall wellbeing tend to unravel alongside it, treatment addresses both the physical symptoms and emotional steadiness together.

Signs your body may be showing

Trouble falling asleep
Easily irritated
Prone to anxiety and tension
Racing thoughts that won't quiet down
Perfectionist tendencies
Loss of appetite under stress
Dry eyes, mouth, and throat
Heart palpitations
Tight shoulders and neck
Sensitive to heat and cold

Dr. Na's Story

From Doctor to Patient

Five egg retrievals. One ectopic pregnancy. One miscarriage. Two embryo transfers cancelled because my wife's uterine lining wasn't thick enough. Six embryo transfers. That's what my wife and I went through over five years before we finally had our daughter.

When we first got the diagnosis that conceiving wouldn't come easily, I honestly wasn't that worried. I'm an acupuncturist — I'd already helped so many patients get pregnant, surely we'd be fine too. I figured with a bit more effort, plus IVF, it would happen soon enough. I had no idea back then just how long it would take, or how many tears it would cost us.

I read every book and paper I could find. I gave myself countless treatments and tried every herbal formula I could think of. There were times my patients succeeded while we didn't, and we'd cry it out and start again. Other times a patient and I would fail together, and we'd pick each other back up and keep going.

Fertility cases range enormously in difficulty, from straightforward to genuinely hard — because some part of this process is still beyond anything we can fully control. Even so, I will give every case everything I have. I want to share with you that overwhelming feeling of meeting your child for the very first time.

BC's Public IVF Funding Program

Good to Know

  • Coverage: up to $19,000 per BC resident, for one funded cycle
  • Eligibility: MSP-covered residents, ages 18–41
  • Application: submitted through a BC fertility clinic
  • Treatment location: designated fertility clinics within BC

Acupuncture isn't covered under this public funding, but we're glad to see fertility treatment becoming more accessible across BC. Greenleaf will be right alongside you throughout that process. Eligibility and funding amounts can change, so please confirm the latest details with your fertility clinic or BC's official program page before moving forward.
