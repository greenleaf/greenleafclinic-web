Vancouver IVF Acupuncture Support

An IVF outcome isn't decided by the embryo alone.
The three months it takes an egg to mature. The few days it takes a uterus to accept an embryo.
Preparing that environment is what acupuncture does.
Since 2007, we've supported couples preparing for IVF in downtown Vancouver.

Same cycle, different degree of readiness.

Blood flow to the uterus and ovaries, endometrial thickness, hormone balance, medication side effects, stress. These all shape your outcome, but they're not always something your fertility clinic can address directly. Acupuncture can help.

Challenges Along the IVF Journey
CASE 01Egg Issues

This comes up when the ovaries aren't responding well enough to hormone stimulation, or when the eggs retrieved don't turn out as hoped.

Poor Ovarian ResponseThe ovaries don't respond well to hormone stimulation.
Declining Egg QualityEgg viability drops with age.
Low Egg Count at RetrievalFew eggs are available to retrieve.
Immature Eggs RetrievedEggs are retrieved before they're fully matured.
Premature OvulationEggs release on their own before retrieval.

How acupuncture & herbal medicine help with egg issues

Acupuncture

  • Increases blood flow to the ovaries
  • Supports follicle development
  • Supports hormone regulation
  • May help improve response to IVF medications
  • Helps regulate the timing of ovulation

Herbal Medicine

  • Si-Wu-Tang family — supports blood circulation
  • Liu-Wei-Di-Huang-Wan — supports egg development
  • Wen-Jing-Tang — supports hormone balance

Formulas are adjusted individually based on pulse diagnosis and consultation. We check your fertility clinic's medication list before prescribing, to avoid any overlap with your IVF medications.

CASE 02Sperm Issues

We look at the count, movement, shape, and viability of the sperm used for fertilization. Nearly half of infertility cases involve a male factor.

Overall HealthSperm aren't healthy enough overall.
MorphologySperm shape falls outside the normal range.
MotilitySperm don't swim forward effectively.
CountSperm count is too low.
ViabilityFew sperm survive the washing process.

How acupuncture & herbal medicine help with sperm issues

  • Supports sperm production
  • Supports hormone balance
  • Helps improve overall sperm parameters
  • Helps regulate the sperm production cycle

Herbal Medicine

  • You-Gui-Wan — supports sperm production and vitality
  • Wu-Zi-Yan-Zong-Wan — supports sperm development and quality
  • Er-Xian-Tang — supports hormone balance and sperm health

Formulas are adjusted individually based on pulse diagnosis and consultation. We check your fertility clinic's medication list before prescribing, to avoid any overlap with your IVF medications.

CASE 04Implantation Failure

The embryo should have implanted but didn't. Sometimes the real cause isn't the embryo itself, but the conditions in the uterus receiving it.

Thin Endometrial LiningThe uterine lining isn't thick enough.
Structural Uterine IssuesThe uterus has growths or other abnormal findings.
Uterus Rejects the EmbryoThe uterus doesn't accept the embryo.
Recurrent Implantation FailureThe embryo repeatedly fails to implant in the uterus.

CASE 05Medication Side Effects

These are the body's reactions to ovarian stimulation and hormone supplementation. Not always severe enough to stop treatment, but hard on day-to-day life.

Ovarian SwellingThe ovaries swell, causing lower-abdominal discomfort.
Hormonal ReactionsHeadaches, hot flashes, and mood swings.
Poor Medication ResponseThe medication doesn't produce the expected response.
Infection RiskThere's an infection risk during the procedure.
Pain and BleedingPain or bleeding around the procedure.

CASE 06Early Pregnancy Instability

Pregnancy is confirmed, but it's still hard to relax. Progesterone support usually continues while you manage how you're feeling.

Pregnancy ComplicationsVarious health issues arise that can affect the pregnancy.
Early MiscarriageThe pregnancy ends before 20 weeks.
Multiple PregnancyCarrying two or more babies at once.
Ectopic PregnancyImplantation outside the uterus, usually in a fallopian tube.

How acupuncture & herbal medicine help with early pregnancy instability

Acupuncture

  • Supports uterine stability in early pregnancy
  • Supports maintaining progesterone levels
  • Increases uterine blood flow to support fetal development
  • Eases morning sickness and nausea
  • May help reduce fatigue and anxiety

Herbal Medicine

  • Xiao-Yao-San — supports hormone balance and relieves stress
  • Gui-Zhi-Fu-Ling-Wan — addresses circulatory stagnation
  • Bu-Zhong-Yi-Qi-Tang — supports fetal development

Ectopic pregnancy is a medical emergency. If you have lower-abdominal pain or bleeding, go to your fertility clinic or the ER before considering acupuncture. Herbal medicine during pregnancy is only prescribed after consulting with your care team.

Before You Get Started

  • Bring your treatment plan to your first visit so we can review it together and time your care around your procedure schedule. Your procedure timeline and medications remain your fertility clinic's call.
  • Bring a list of the medications and supplements you're currently taking so we can check them before prescribing any herbal formula.
  • If you'd like treatment on transfer day, let us know your transfer time in advance so we can reserve time before and after.
  • Individual responses to treatment vary. We can't guarantee any specific outcome.

MSP covers a set number of acupuncture visits per year, and Extended Health Insurance coverage depends on your specific plan. Ask us at booking whether direct billing is available.
