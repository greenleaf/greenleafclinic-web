Healing After Miscarriage

Dr. Byoungjin Na, Dr.TCM — Director, Greenleaf Acupuncture & Herb Clinic

A few years ago, a patient came to our clinic after experiencing a miscarriage. It was an incredibly painful and emotional time, both physically and mentally. What followed was a journey of gentle healing, careful support, and ultimately, hope renewed.

"Dr. Na gave me an emergency session before I took the pill, and it greatly helped to smooth the process. The Greenleaf team was there every step of the way, through my miscarriage, recovery, IVF, and pregnancy.

Now I'm 26 weeks pregnant with a baby boy. I couldn't have done this without them."

Her courage to share this story reminds us how important gentle, supportive care can be, especially during and after such a difficult experience.

What happens after a miscarriage

After a miscarriage, the body goes through dramatic changes, hormonally, physically, and emotionally. Western medicine's focus in this window is straightforward: making sure all tissue has passed and preventing infection. Traditional Eastern medicine looks at the same window as a critical phase of recovery, one where the uterus, circulation, and emotional balance all need to be restored carefully, preparing the body not just for the weeks ahead, but for future fertility.

Herbal medicine for each stage of recovery

Traditional herbal formulas are selected based on the stage of recovery, each one supporting a different part of the body's natural healing process.

3 — The rebuilding stage Gui Pi Tang (歸脾湯) Replenishes energy and blood, stabilizes emotions, improves sleep, and supports the nervous system — key elements for future fertility and wellbeing Ren Shen (人參, ginseng) — strengthens vitality; Huang Qi (黃芪, astragalus) — boosts energy and immune function; Dang Gui (當歸) — nourishes blood; Long Yan Rou (龍眼肉, longan fruit) — calms the mind; Suan Zao Ren (酸棗仁, ziziphus seed) — improves sleep, reduces anxiety; Fu Ling (茯苓, poria) — calms the spirit, strengthens digestion

Each formula builds on the one before it, so the body heals completely at every level, from physical clearing through to emotional restoration.

Many patients describe a sense of calm and grounding after treatment, a feeling that the body and mind are reconnecting again.

Preparing for the next chapter

Once cycles stabilize and energy returns, ongoing treatment shifts toward strengthening the reproductive system, supporting either natural conception or IVF preparation. Recovery over this stretch tends to move on a few fronts at once: the uterine lining rebuilds and hormones rebalance as cycles regulate; grief gets acknowledged and stress gets processed as hope returns; the body becomes stronger and more resilient for conception; and trust in the body's own wisdom is slowly restored.

Healing after loss is possible, and gentle, attentive care can transform recovery into renewal — not only of the body, but of hope itself.

When to seek support

Consider Traditional Eastern Medicine support if you
  • Have recently experienced a miscarriage and want comprehensive recovery support
  • Are experiencing prolonged bleeding or incomplete clearing
  • Feel physically weak, cold, or depleted after miscarriage
  • Have irregular cycles or difficulty recovering hormonally
  • Are emotionally struggling and need gentle, holistic support
  • Want to prepare your body for future conception or IVF
  • Are looking for an integrative approach alongside Western medical care

Healing after miscarriage takes time, compassion, and the right support. At Greenleaf Acupuncture Clinic, we provide gentle, comprehensive care through every stage of recovery. Our integrative approach combines acupuncture with customized herbal formulas to help your body heal completely: physically, hormonally, and emotionally. You don't have to go through this alone. We're here to support you every step of the way.
