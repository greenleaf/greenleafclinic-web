Together

Massage and acupuncture, in one clinic.

Most Vancouver clinics offer both. Very few have the two practitioners reading the same chart, actively working each other's progress into the next visit. Here is specifically how each treatment makes the other one work better.

How massage supports your acupuncture care

  • Loosens tight, guarded muscle first, so acupuncture points are easier to reach and respond faster
  • Increases local circulation, helping tissue hold the change acupuncture starts
  • Gives you relief you can feel same-day, while the acupuncture plan builds its effect over weeks
  • Flags mechanical restrictions your acupuncturist can then target with specific points

How acupuncture supports your massage care

  • Calms the nervous-system pattern that keeps muscle re-tightening after you leave the table
  • Treats pain that moves around or has no single spot to massage
  • Addresses the sleep, stress or digestive pattern that is keeping tissue guarded
  • Reaches nerve-type pain — numbness, tingling, sciatica — that pressure alone can aggravate

Questions

Questions patients ask before they book.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book Registered Massage Therapy directly. Most extended health plans do not require a referral, and ICBC does not require one either.

Do you direct bill my insurance?

Yes — most major extended health plans through TELUS eClaims and Pacific Blue Cross. You pay only the portion your plan does not cover, at the front desk.

Is massage covered after a car accident?

Yes. ICBC pre-approves 12 massage therapy visits in the first 12 weeks after a crash, and they are counted separately from acupuncture. Bring your claim number and PHN — we handle the billing.

How is this different from a spa massage?

A Registered Massage Therapist is a regulated health professional. Your visit starts with an assessment, gets documented in your chart, and is eligible for insurance and ICBC billing. The goal is a specific clinical change, not an hour of relaxation.

Can I do acupuncture and massage on the same day?

Yes, and for a lot of conditions we suggest it. Both practitioners work from the same chart, so nothing gets repeated and nothing gets missed.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable. Your therapist explains which areas will be worked on and how draping works before anything starts, and you decide what you are comfortable with.
